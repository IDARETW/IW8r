# IW8 Radiant Suite: roadmap to a full suite

Revised 2026-11-10 (owner request: a fuller roadmap with more features and deeper IW8 map-asset support and editing). The
earlier version is kept in [archive/ROADMAP_2026-11-10_before_revamp.md](archive/ROADMAP_2026-11-10_before_revamp.md). Item
numbers from that version are unchanged; every new item has a new number.

Target: an editor that gives MW2019 (Replay 1.20.4.7623265) mappers what iw3xo-radiant gives CoD4 mappers, and then goes
past it for IW8. That means:

- the stock Radiant editing set, a textured and lit live preview, asset browsers, one-key build / deploy / load, live link;
- every IW8 map asset a mapper touches can be **browsed, inspected, placed, edited and built**: materials, images, models,
  effects, sounds, lights and IES profiles, probes, vision and fog, decals, scriptables, dynamic entities, spawns, nav data
  and scripts;
- stock maps can be opened, changed and rebuilt, and older CoD maps can be brought in through the converter.

Sources: [FEATURE_MATRIX.md](FEATURE_MATRIX.md) (row-level status),
[research/01](research/01_iw3xo_feature_audit.md) (iw3xo inventory),
[research/09](research/09_iw8_render_features.md) (IW8 render systems),
[research/10](research/10_iw8_entity_inventory.md) (IW8 entity classes),
[research/15](research/15_iw8_extraction_contract.md), [research/17](research/17_x64zt_buildextraction.md) and
[research/18](research/18_x64zt_stock_assets.md) (build route).

**How to read this.** Each layer is a coherent tier of the suite and has a mapper-facing exit test. A layer depends only on
the layers marked as its prerequisites. Several layers can be worked in parallel.
- Status words: **done** (built and covered by tests; "offline" means not yet seen in game), **partial**, **open**,
  **paused**.
- Size: **S** = one focused session, **M** = a few sessions, **L** = a week or more of sessions, **XL** = multi-week or open
  research.
- Flags:
  - **R** = blocked on research (an unknown in the game, x64-zt or ACTS).
  - **U** = needs an owner decision or owner action: an in-game test, or writing to the game install.
- Converter changes go on the local x64-zt fork branch `iw8radiant/build-extraction` (never pushed).
