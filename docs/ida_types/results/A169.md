- Lead for A092 (not applied): the dynamic initializer for `PartyState_14C73AE40` constructs members at lobby offsets 96, 136, 140, 144, 2192, 99792, 310960, 361984, 362000 and 373312. Those fit the PartyData layout; the offsets 96-144 fall inside `migrateData` and `requestBuffer` and were not checked further.
